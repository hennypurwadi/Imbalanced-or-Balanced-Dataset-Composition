## Assessing the Impact of Documenting Dataset Composition for Model Training—Whether Imbalanced or Balanced—on AI Model Transparency and Fairness

### Examines Bias and Unfairness in Explainable Artificial Intelligence (XAI) Resulting from Imbalanced Training Datasets

### ABSTRACT

In the field of Responsible AI, to ensure transparency and fairness is very important for developing ethical AI systems. Traditionally, Explainable AI (XAI) has concentrated on explaining model decisions, but it is equally important to also understand and document the dataset composition used during model training. This paper analyzes how important the dataset documentation into XAI practices is, aiming for comprehensive transparency at both data and model, instead of only focusing on the model explainability. This research underscores the need to shift from a purely model-focused approach to amore focusing on a training dataset composition view for AI transparency and accountability, since Machine learning models may fail to predict correctly from imbalanced training data. This paper examines the impact of dataset composition, whether balanced or imbalance, on avoiding bias and unfairness of Model’s prediction. Detailed documentation of dataset structure, sources, and imbalances helps improve model interpretability and trustworthiness. This paper reveals that documenting datasets effectively mitigates biases, enhances fairness, and strengthens stakeholder trust in AI systems.
