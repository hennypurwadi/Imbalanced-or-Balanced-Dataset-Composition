Introduction:
This Research proposes the incorporation of dataset characteristics to be documented into Responsible AI and Explainable AI (XAI) practices, 
especially the homogeneity composition, whether the dataset is balanced or imbalanced, 
and data sparsity, whether small or large samples, which are critical approach for transparency at both the data and model levels

Research Questions:
1.How does the documentation of dataset homogeneity composition (in this case balance vs. imbalanced) impact the AI model transparency and fairness in decision-making systems? 
2. What is the relationship between data sparsity (like dataset’s size) and the bias evaluation in AI models, when the training data is imbalanced ?
3. How do small sample sizes mislead bias evaluation, and what are the implications for models trained on imbalanced datasets?

Literature Review:
The previous studies founded that the imbalance ratio (IR) influences the performance and stability of the model, especially in model like Logistic Regression, Decision Trees, and Support Vector Classifiers.
The previous study also warned that too small sample sizes can create significant bias. 

Terminology and Definition
These are performance metrics used in this study, which are Accuracy, Precision, Recall, F1-score and Area Under the Curve/ AUC.

Methodology: 
- Synthetic dataset used for both quantitative analysis and comparative evaluations using Logistic Regression.
- Small Balanced dataset consisted of ten thousands dataset, versus Large Balanced dataset consisted of 1 million dataset, then investigate with LIME (local interpretable model-agnostic explanations).
- Small Imbalanced dataset consisted of ten thousands dataset, versus Large Imbalanced dataset consisted of 1 million dataset, then investigate with LIME (local interpretable model-agnostic explanations). 

Result Findings were in-line with previous studies' findings:
-The research also found how small sample sizes can provide a false sense of fairness. This bias becomes obvious when the size of the dataset increase.
-Models trained on imbalanced datasets showed bias, favouring the majority class and ignore the minority classes. 
-This study recommends the documentation of dataset characteristics into Responsible AI and Explainable AI (XAI) practices. 
Documenting dataset composition increased the AI model transparency and fairness and gain more trust from the stakeholders about the AI systems.

Discussion of Limitation
-This study were not perfect and has several limitations.

Recommendation for Future Research
-Could use Over-sampling or Under-sampling method to deal with Imbalanced dataset.
-Evaluate with other models.
- Using real data instead of synthetic dataset.
